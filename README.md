
#  Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard — Power BI

This project presents an interactive **Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** on the popular **Telco Customer Churn Dataset**.

The goal of this dashboard is to identify **why customers are churning**, which customer segments are at high risk, and what business factors influence churn.

---

## objective

To analyze customer behavior and uncover key drivers of churn based on:

* Contract type
* Internet service
* Payment method
* Tenure
* Monthly charges
* Senior citizen status

---

##  Key KPIs

* **Total Customers:** 7043
* **Churn Customers:** 1869
* **Churn Rate:** 27%
* **Total Revenue:** 16.06M

These KPIs give a quick snapshot of the churn situation.

---

##  Insights from the Dashboard

### 🔹 Contract Type Impact

Customers on **Month-to-month contracts** show significantly higher churn compared to one-year and two-year contracts.
 Long-term contracts reduce churn.

### 🔹 Internet Service Influence

Customers using **Fiber optic internet** have the highest churn rate.
 Indicates possible pricing or service quality issues.

### 🔹 Payment Method Behavior

Customers paying via **Electronic check** churn the most.
 Automatic payment methods (bank transfer, credit card) retain customers better.

### 🔹 Tenure Trend

Churn is highest in the **early months** of customer tenure and decreases over time.
 The first few months are critical for customer retention.

### 🔹 Monthly Charges Effect

Higher monthly charges are associated with increased churn.
 Price sensitivity plays a major role.

### 🔹 Senior Citizen Segment

Senior citizens show a noticeable share of churn.
 This segment may need targeted support or plans.

## Tools Used

* **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**
* Data transformation using Power Query

---

##  Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Encourage customers to move to long-term contracts.
2. Investigate service/pricing issues for fiber optic users.
3. Promote automatic payment methods.
4. Focus retention efforts during the first 6 months.
5. Review pricing strategy for high monthly charge plans.
6. Provide tailored plans for senior citizens.
